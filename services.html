Our Services.

Comprehensive dental care for your entire family. From routine cleanings to advanced procedures, we're here to help you maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

Dental Exam & X-ray

Our comprehensive oral examination is the foundation of preventive dental care. During your visit, our experienced dentists will thoroughly assess your oral health, checking for cavities, gum disease, and other potential issues.

What's Included:

  • Complete oral health examination
  • Full set of digital X-rays for accurate diagnosis
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Gum disease assessment
  • Personalized treatment plan
  • Discussion of any concerns or questions

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy is a procedure designed to save your natural tooth when the pulp becomes infected or damaged. Our gentle approach ensures your comfort while preserving your tooth and relieving pain.

What's Included:

  • Local anesthesia for pain-free treatment
  • Removal of infected or damaged pulp
  • Thorough cleaning and disinfection of root canals
  • Sealing of the tooth to prevent future infection
  • Temporary or permanent filling
  • Post-treatment care instructions

Occlusal Night Guard

Protect your teeth from grinding and clenching with a custom-fitted night guard. This comfortable appliance prevents tooth wear, reduces jaw pain, and helps you wake up refreshed without the effects of nighttime bruxism.

What's Included:

  • Evaluation for teeth grinding and TMJ issues
  • Custom impressions for perfect fit
  • Durable, comfortable night guard
  • Fitting and adjustment session
  • Care and cleaning instructions
  • Follow-up to ensure effectiveness

Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth discreetly with clear aligner therapy. These virtually invisible, removable aligners gradually shift your teeth into their ideal position without the need for traditional metal braces, giving you the confidence to smile throughout your treatment.

What's Included:

  • Comprehensive orthodontic evaluation
  • Digital 3D treatment planning
  • Custom-fabricated clear aligners
  • Regular progress monitoring appointments
  • Refinement aligners if needed
  • Retainers to maintain your new smile
